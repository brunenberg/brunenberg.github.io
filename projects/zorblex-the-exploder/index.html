Unity Game “Zorblex the Exploder”

Zorblex the Exploder on Itch.io!

Overview 🗺️

In the first six weeks of the minor I did (Game Design and Development), the challenge was to create a 2D game in Unity in a team of around five people. The team consisted of two developers (including myself), two artists and one game designer. We agreed on making a game around the concept of rocket jumping, which is usually a concept utilized more often in 3D games like Quake and Team Fortress 2. In the game, the goal is to finish levels with the least amount of bullets as possible.

Technologies Used 🛠️

  • Unity/Unity 2D
  • C#
  • Paint.NET (creating/editing some of the sprites)
  • Git/GitHub
