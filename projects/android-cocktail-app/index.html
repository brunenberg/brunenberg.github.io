Android Cocktail App

Overview 🗺️

This was an app I made with a duo partner for the subject “Mobile Development - Android”. It features a library of cocktails and their recipes, these are retrieved via the TheCocktailDB API.

Technologies Used 🛠️

  • Android Studio
  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Hilt
  • Ktor (API calls)
  • Coil (image loading)
  • Coroutines
